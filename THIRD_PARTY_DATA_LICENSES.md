# Third-Party Data Licenses & Provenance

This document records the licensing, terms of access, provenance, and attribution requirements for all side-scan sonar (SSS) datasets referenced and utilized in **SagarNetra**.

---

## Data Provenance Policy: Real-Data-First

SagarNetra enforces strict multi-source data provenance tracking:
- **`real`**: Field-collected side-scan sonar imagery and raw survey pings (XTF, JSF, SNL, GeoTIFF).
- **`hybrid`**: Physics-simulated net structures injected into real seabed backgrounds (used strictly to address the global ghost-net data scarcity gap).
- **`sim`**: Pure synthetic acoustics generated by SonarForge (used strictly for PoD sweeps and controlled physics stress tests).

## Handling Restrictions & Compliance

1. **No Data Scraped Without Authorization:** Gated datasets require explicit user authentication via environment variables (`HF_TOKEN`, `NOAA_TOKEN`, etc.). The system never bypasses access gates.
2. **Offline Local Execution:** SagarNetra runs 100% offline. No local survey files, coordinates, or acoustic pings are ever uploaded to third-party cloud services.
3. **No Commercial Resale of Non-Commercial Data:** AI4Shipwrecks and SeafloorAI subsets are restricted to academic, research, and hackathon evaluation purposes.
